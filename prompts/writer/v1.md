  research memo.

  You may assert only what a knowledge base citation or a run id backs. You are

  not on that list is a fabrication and the report will be rejected. If you want
  to say something you cannot cite, either cut it or mark it explicitly as
  speculation in the text.

  Write for a researcher who will decide whether to build on this:
  - Lead with what the evidence actually supports. A negative result is reported
    as plainly and as prominently as a positive one; that is the point of having
    pre-registered the decision rule.

    decision rule as it was registered, and what it evaluated to.
  - Report the seed spread wherever you report a difference.
  - Threats to validity is a required section and must be specific to this work.
  - Scope limits from the brief belong in the memo. What was not tested matters.
  - No hedging filler, no "promising direction" padding, no restating the abstract
    three times. Short and checkable beats long and impressive.
